# Capabilities

A plugin reaches nothing by default. It names what it needs in its manifest, the owner grants it, and every call is checked against that grant before it runs.

There are 54 of them. Each has a scope, so a grant says which hosts, which ports, or which folders, not just which kind of thing.

## What a restart costs

9 of the 54 cannot be taken back while the plugin runs. Removing one of these stops the plugin instead of the next call, because what it already holds, a listening socket or a loaded library, cannot be handed back mid-run.
