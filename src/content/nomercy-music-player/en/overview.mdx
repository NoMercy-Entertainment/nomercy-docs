# What is the NoMercy Music Player

[`nomercy-music-player`](/nomercy-music-player/api/factory) is the headless, event-driven audio player that powers music on NoMercy TV. It plays audio through a swappable backend — the Web Audio backend for sample-accurate crossfade, or the native media element — and is HLS-ready for adaptive audio streams.

It handles the genuinely hard part for you — the hand-off between two tracks, as a sample-accurate crossfade or a gapless transition — alongside lyric sync and a full equalizer chain, and then gets out of your way.

The important word is *headless*. There is no UI in the box and no layout you have to accept: you get a working audio pipeline wired to plain events and methods, and everything on screen is yours to build.

## What you get

- `NMMusicPlayer<T>`, created through the [`nmMPlayer(id)`](/nomercy-music-player/api/factory) factory and generic over your own track type.
- Sample-accurate [crossfade](/nomercy-music-player/crossfade) and gapless playback, behind a swappable [audio backend](/nomercy-music-player/backends/overview).
- [Lyric sync](/nomercy-music-player/lyrics), a ten-band [equalizer](/nomercy-music-player/equalizer), and spectrum data for visualisers.
- HLS-ready adaptive audio streaming.
- A fully typed [event map](/nomercy-music-player/events) and a plugin for every optional capability.
- Full TypeScript types for every method, event, and option.

## Where it sits

The generic parts (queue, auth, plugins, i18n, storage) live in `nomercy-player-core` underneath, so this package stays focused on what makes audio hard. Plugin authors type against `IMusicPlayer<T>` rather than the concrete class.
