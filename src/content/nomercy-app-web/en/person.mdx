# People

A person page shows who someone is, and what of theirs your server holds.

The biography and photograph come from [TheMovieDB](https://www.themoviedb.org). The list of titles is your own library, so a long career shows only the part you have.

## Getting there

From the cast list on any [title details](/nomercy-app-web/info) page, or from People in the sidebar, which lists everyone your library involves.

::Troubleshooting

**A person page will not open.** The details are fetched while you open the page, so a server that cannot reach the metadata service shows nothing rather than a stale page. Try again once it is back.

**Someone is missing from a cast list.** Cast comes with the title's metadata. Refreshing that title from the dashboard fetches it again.

::
