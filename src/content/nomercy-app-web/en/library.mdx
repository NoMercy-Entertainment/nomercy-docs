# Inside a library

A library is a grid of what it holds. Each card is a film, a series, or an album, with its artwork and enough to recognise it.

## Finding something in a big library

Large libraries are browsed by first letter. Pick a letter and you get that page of titles, rather than scrolling through everything.

Titles that do not start with a letter are grouped under `#`.

Knowing the name already? [Search](/nomercy-app-web/search) is quicker than either.

## Opening a title

A card opens its details page: what it is, who is in it, and what you can play. See [Title details](/nomercy-app-web/info).

::Troubleshooting

**A title is missing.** The server may not have scanned it yet. A scan is started from the dashboard, on [Libraries](/nomercy-app-web/dashboard/libraries).

**A title is here with the wrong name or artwork.** The match went to the wrong title. Fixing it is a dashboard job: see [Metadata](/nomercy-media-server/media/metadata).

::
