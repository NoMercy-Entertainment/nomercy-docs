# Your profile

The profile screen is the way into everything you can change. On a phone it is a tab; on a desktop it is the menu behind your avatar in the top bar.

## What is on it

**You.** Your name and picture, as your account carries them.

**The server.** Which one you are pointed at, and whether it is reachable. Switching servers happens here.

**Your account.** The way through to display, subtitle and device settings.

**Signing out.** Ends the session on this device only. Your other devices stay signed in.

## Switching servers

Only when your account can reach more than one. It changes what you browse, without signing you out.
