# Your devices

Every device you sign in on appears here: phones, browsers, televisions. It is your own list, not the server's.

## What each row shows

The device's name, whether it is online or asleep, and when it was last seen if it is not here now.

Naming a device when you first sign in on it is what keeps this list readable. See [Display](/nomercy-app-web/preferences/display).

## Forgetting one

The button on the right removes a device, after asking once. Use it for a browser you will not open again, or a phone you no longer have.

Forgetting is not permanent: signing in on that device again puts it back. What it does is end its access now.

The list updates itself when it changes, so a device removed on one screen disappears on the others without a reload.

::Troubleshooting

**The list is empty.** Devices appear once they have signed in. A browser that has only ever been signed out is not in it.

**A device I own says asleep.** It is registered and not currently connected. A television with the screen off usually looks like this, and casting to it asks it to wake.

::
