# Display

Everything about how the app looks and behaves on this device.

## This device

**Device name.** What this device is called in your device list. Naming it now saves guessing later, when the list has four browsers in it.

**Language.** The language the app speaks. It also decides which translated titles and details you get.

## Theme

**Colour scheme.** Light, dark, or following the system.

**Theme colours.** The palette the app uses.

**Colour from artwork.** With this on, a title's own artwork tints the screen you are looking at.

## Screensaver

How long the app waits before starting its screensaver, or off.

## Playback and browsing

**Fullscreen on play.** Start playback in fullscreen rather than in the page.

**Open the latest season.** Open a series on its most recent season rather than the first.

**Backdrops.** Show the large background images. Turning them off makes for a plainer, lighter screen.

**Carousel buttons.** Draw the row arrows over the artwork instead of beside it.

**Progress colours.** Colour the progress marks on cards.

These are per device, so a phone can be plain and a desktop lavish.
