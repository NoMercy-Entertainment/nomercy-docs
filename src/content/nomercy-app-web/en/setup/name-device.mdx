# Naming your device

The first time you sign in on a new phone, TV, browser or computer, NoMercy asks what to call it.

## Why it asks

Your account works on many devices at once, and several features need to tell them apart.

The name appears when you cast from your phone to your TV, in the list of devices in your settings, and in your server's activity so you can see what was watched where.

A device with no useful name makes all of those harder to read.

## Choosing a name

You get suggestions based on what the device appears to be. Pick one, or type your own.

Say where it is rather than what it is. "Living room TV" is easier to pick out of a list later than "Android TV" when you own two.

## Skipping

You can skip. The device gets a generic name.

The cost is a device list that is harder to read, and casting targets that are harder to tell apart.

## Changing it later

The name is not permanent. Change it in your settings under devices, on any device, at any time.
