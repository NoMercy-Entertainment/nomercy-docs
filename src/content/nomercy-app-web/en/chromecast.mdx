# Casting

The cast button appears in the player when there is something on your network to cast to.

## Casting to a Chromecast

Press the cast button, pick the device, and playback moves there. The app becomes the remote: play, pause, seek, volume, subtitles and audio all still work from the screen in your hand.

Chapters travel with it, so the chapter bar on the television is the same one you had here.

## Sending to your TV app

A television running the NoMercy app is also a target. That is not casting in the Chromecast sense: the TV plays from the server itself, and the app tells it what to play.

The advantage is that the TV does the work, so closing the browser does not stop the film.

::Troubleshooting

**No cast button.** Nothing castable was found on this network. Casting discovery needs the device and the receiver on the same network, and some networks block the discovery traffic between wireless clients.

**The TV is listed but does not start.** It may be asleep. The app asks it to wake, and a device that does not answer is reported rather than silently ignored.

**Volume changes nothing.** On a television connected over HDMI, the volume belongs to the television or the amplifier, not to the app.

::
