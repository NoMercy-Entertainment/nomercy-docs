# Connecting to your server

Open [app.nomercy.tv](https://app.nomercy.tv), sign in with your NoMercy account, and the app lists the servers you can reach.

## What the app connects to

Your server has its own address, issued when it was set up. The app connects straight to it, so the media never travels through anything of ours.

At home the app reaches your server across your own network, which is the fastest path. From elsewhere it reaches the same server over the internet. You do not choose between them; the app uses what works.

## First time on a device

Three short steps, and only the first time:

1. Sign in.
2. Name this device, so you recognise it later in the device list.
3. Pick a server, if you can reach more than one.

See [Name this device](/nomercy-app-web/setup/name-device) and [Selecting a server](/nomercy-app-web/setup/select-server).

## Switching servers

From your profile menu. It is a separate action from signing out, so you keep your session.

::Troubleshooting

**The list is empty.** Usually a different account, or a server that has not finished its own setup. See [Selecting a server](/nomercy-app-web/setup/select-server).

**The server is listed but will not connect.** See [Server offline](/nomercy-app-web/setup/server-offline).

**It works away from home but not at home.** Your browser is holding back a request from a public page into your own network, and it wants your permission first. See [Web app can't reach my server on my home network](/nomercy-app-web/troubleshooting#web-app-cant-reach-my-server-on-my-home-network).

::
