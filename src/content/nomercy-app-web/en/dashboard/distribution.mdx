# Distribution

Encoding can be spread across more than one machine. This screen shows the machines that have joined and the work assigned to them.

The screen appears only when distributed encoding is switched on. A single-machine server has nothing to show here and is sent to the system overview instead.

## What a worker shows

**Cores and threads available.** What it can take on.

**Graphics devices and free slots.** How many accelerated encodes it can run at once.

**Utilisation.** How busy its graphics hardware is now.

**Failures.** How many jobs it has failed. A worker that keeps failing is worth looking at rather than waiting on.

**Last seen.** Workers keep a heartbeat going. One that stops sending it drops out of the list.

**Cooldown.** A worker that has just failed is rested before more work goes to it, and the remaining time shows here.

## The work

Tasks show as a total and a count of active ones, with which worker each is assigned to.
