# Devices

Every device that has signed in to this server, across all its users. Your own devices are a separate, smaller list under [Your devices](/nomercy-app-web/preferences/devices).

## What a device shows

Its name, its kind, who it belongs to, whether it is connected now, and when it was last seen. Opening one shows what it has been doing.

## Tidying up

**Remove offline devices** clears everything not currently connected, after confirming. It is the usual tidy-up on a server that has collected a year of browsers.

**Clear activity logs** drops the recorded activity for devices rather than the devices themselves.

Removing a device ends its access now. Signing in on it again puts it back, so this is a tidy-up rather than a ban.
