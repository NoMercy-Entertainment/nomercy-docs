# Users

Who may use your server. People are invited by their NoMercy account; no password is created here.

## Inviting someone

Invite by email address, and choose which libraries they get while you are there.

They need a NoMercy account with that address. Once invited, your server appears in their server list and they sign in with the account they already have.

## What you can grant

**Access to content.** The basic switch. Without it, an invited account can sign in and see nothing.

**Optical access.** Lets them use the disc drive: probe a disc and play from it.

**Manage the server.** Gives them the dashboard, apart from the parts reserved to the owner.

**Audio transcoding** and **video transcoding.** Whether playback that needs converting on the fly is allowed for them. Turning these off means they get what plays as it is, and nothing that would put the machine to work.

## Library access

Either all libraries, or a chosen few. The picker appears when you choose a few.

This is what makes a shared server workable: a household member can have everything, and a friend one shelf.

## Removing someone

Removing takes away their access. Nothing of theirs is deleted from disk, and their own account is untouched.

## The owner

One account owns the server, and that does not move. The owner can do everything a manager can, plus the owner-only screens such as plugins.
