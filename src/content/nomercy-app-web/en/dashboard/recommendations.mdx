# Recommendations

Titles your server does not have, suggested from what it does. Films, series and anime are worked out separately, because what makes a good suggestion differs.

## Using it

Open a kind, browse the suggestions, open one to see why it was suggested and what it is. Anything you decide to keep is added the way any title is.

Suggestions are worked out from your library, so they change as it grows.

## When the list looks thin

The diagnostics view explains what the recommender had to work with. A small or one-sided library gives it little to reason from, and the honest answer is usually that rather than a fault.
