# Specials

A special is a collection you build: films and episodes, in a deliberate order. It is the grouping no metadata source can give you, such as a viewing order that crosses two series.

Everyone with access to the server sees it. That is what separates it from a playlist, which is personal.

## Building one

Give it a name and a description, then add items and put them in order.

Artwork is yours to set: a poster, a backdrop and a logo. Without them a special still works, and looks plainer beside the rest of the library.

## Editing

Open a special to change its details, its artwork or its running order. Rescanning it makes the server check the items are still there.
