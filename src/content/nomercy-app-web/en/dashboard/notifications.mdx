# Notifications

The notifications the server has sent, and a way to send one of your own.

## Sending one

Choose a recipient, or everyone, then give it a title and a message.

This is for telling people something they would otherwise find out by being surprised: the server going down for an hour, a library being reorganised.

## The list

What has been sent, most recent first. Opening one shows it in full.

The list can be cleared, which removes the record rather than recalling anything.

## Where they arrive

Wherever the person has notifications enabled: the app, and a phone that has allowed them. A device that was away gets what it missed the next time it connects.
