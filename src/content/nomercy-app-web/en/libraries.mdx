# Libraries

A library is a shelf: films in one, series in another, music in its own. What you see is what the server owner has made and given your account access to.

Opening Libraries with only one library takes you straight into it.

## Other ways to browse

The same media is reachable by more than one path, because different moods want different routes.

| Route | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| Genres | Everything filed under one genre |
| Collections | Film series held together, such as a trilogy |
| Specials | Hand-made collections the server owner built |
| People | Cast and crew, and what they are in |
| Favorites | What you have liked |
| My lists | Your own playlists |

Anime adds three more of its own: themes, demographics and seasons. A season here is the broadcast season, not a season of one series.
