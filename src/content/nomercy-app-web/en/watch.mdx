# Watching

Press play on a [title details](/nomercy-app-web/info) page and the player takes the screen.

## The controls

The usual set: play and pause, a progress bar you can drag, volume, fullscreen, and pickers for subtitles, audio and quality.

Controls fade while you watch and come back when you move the pointer or press a key. On a touch screen, a tap brings them back.

Volume is remembered between sessions, so a player opened later starts where you left it rather than at full.

## Keyboard

| Key | Does |
|-----|------|
| Space | Play and pause |
| Left and right | Seek |
| Up and down | Volume |
| `m` | Mute |
| `f` | Fullscreen |
| `n` and `p` | Next and previous item |
| `v` | Cycle subtitle track |
| `b` | Cycle audio track |
| `[` and `]` | Slower and faster |
| `=` | Back to normal speed |

Holding a modifier with left or right changes how far a seek jumps: shift for a nudge, alt for a few seconds, control for a minute.

Media keys on a keyboard work too, and so do the coloured buttons on a TV remote.

## Skipping an intro

Where the server has marked an intro, a recap or the credits, a skip button appears while that part is playing. Press it, or let it play; nothing is skipped without you.

The server's own marks are used where it has them, and chapter marks in the file where it does not. Marking is done by content analysis, described in [Content segments](/nomercy-api/rest/content-segments).

## Carrying on

Progress is saved as you watch, on the server rather than in the browser. That is why a film paused on the laptop resumes on the TV.

## Casting

The player has a cast button when a receiver is on your network. See [Casting](/nomercy-app-web/chromecast).

::Troubleshooting

**Playback stutters or drops quality.** The server may be transcoding to suit this device. See [Quality](/nomercy-app-web/quality).

**Subtitles do not appear.** They may not be selected, or the file may carry none. See [Subtitles](/nomercy-app-web/subtitles).

**Nothing plays at all.** See [Common app issues](/nomercy-app-web/troubleshooting).

::
