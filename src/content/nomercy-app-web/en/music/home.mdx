# Music

Music has its own screen, reached from the sidebar. It opens on what you listen to: favourites, favourite artists and albums, and your playlists.

## Browsing

Artists, albums and genres each have their own list, browsed by first letter. Names that do not start with a letter are grouped under `#`.

Music search is separate from video search, so a band and a film with the same name stay out of each other's way.

## The player

The player sits along the bottom and stays there while you browse, so opening an album does not stop the music.

Lyrics show where they are available, following along with the track when the file's lyrics are timed.

## It follows you

Playback lives on the server, not in the tab. Start something on the laptop and the phone picks up the same queue at the same position.

That is also what lets a phone drive what plays on a speaker or a television, without either knowing the other's address.

::Troubleshooting

**An artist or album has no picture.** Artwork is fetched separately from the music itself, and one that could not be found stays blank rather than blocking the track.

**A track will not play.** Check the album page: a track the server cannot read shows there too.

::
