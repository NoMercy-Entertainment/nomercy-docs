# Artists

An artist page shows their albums and tracks as your server holds them, with the artwork and details that came with the music.

## From here

Play everything, open an album, or like the artist so they show up under your favourites.

Liking is per account. Another person on the same server has their own favourites.

## Editing an artist

Someone who administers the server can correct an artist's details or replace the picture from this page. Everyone else sees it as it is.
