# Playlists

A playlist is yours. You build it, you order it, and nobody else on the server sees it.

## Building one

Make a playlist, then add tracks to it from an album or an artist page. Tracks are added and removed one at a time, and the order is yours to set.

A playlist can have its own cover.

## Music and video are separate

A music playlist holds tracks. A video playlist holds films, series, episodes and specials. Neither can hold the other, so a mixed list is not possible.

Video playlists live under My lists, alongside your libraries.

## Playlist or special

A playlist is personal. A [special](/nomercy-media-server/media/specials) belongs to the server and everyone with access sees it.

Build a special when the order is part of the library, such as a viewing order across two series. Build a playlist when it is just what you feel like.
