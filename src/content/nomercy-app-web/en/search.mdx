# Search

Search looks at your server: what it holds, not what exists in the world.

Video and music are searched separately, so a band and a film with the same name do not crowd each other out.

## What it matches

Titles, and the people in them. Accents and case are ignored, so a query typed the easy way still finds the title spelled the proper way.

Results come back grouped: the best match first, then the rest by kind.

## When nothing comes back

Either the server does not hold it, or it holds it under a different name than you expect. A film matched to the wrong entry carries the wrong name, and searching the name you expect then finds nothing. Browsing the library will show you what it actually calls itself.
