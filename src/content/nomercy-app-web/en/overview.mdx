# What the app is

The app is where you watch. Your server is where your media lives. They are two different things, and knowing which is which explains most of what follows.

```text
https://app.nomercy.tv
```

Open that, sign in, pick your server, and you are watching.

## Nothing of yours passes through us

The app is a page. When it plays something, it fetches it from your server directly. The video goes from your machine to your screen.

That is why the app works on your home network with the internet down, and why a big library costs you nothing in hosting.

## One app, every server you are allowed on

Sign in once and the app lists the servers you have access to: your own, and any a friend has invited you to. Switching between them is a menu, not a second account.

## What runs where

**Your server** holds the media, the library, the users, and everything the dashboard changes. It does the encoding.

**The app** shows what the server describes. Screens are built from what the server sends, so a server that gains a feature shows it in the app you already have.

**Your account** lives with the NoMercy identity service, so one account gets you into every server you are allowed on.

## The dashboard is part of the app

Administering the server happens in the same app, under Dashboard. If you own a server you get it; if you were invited to someone else's, you do not.
