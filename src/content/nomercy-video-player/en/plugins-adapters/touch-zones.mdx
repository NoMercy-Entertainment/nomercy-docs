# Plugin: Touch Zones

`cls TouchZonesPlugin` (id `str touch-zones`) lays a transparent grid over the picture and turns taps on it into playback. One tap toggles playback in the middle or puts the controls away at the sides. Two taps seek, or move the level on a phone.

The grid sits at z-index 10, above the video element and under `cls DesktopUiPlugin`'s overlay at 20. So it catches a tap on the picture without swallowing one on a control bar.

Every zone listens for `str click`, which a mouse raises as readily as a finger. The two plugins coordinate through the player's `str activity` event rather than through each other.

## Options

| Option | Type | Default | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `key doubleTapThreshold` | `cls number` | `300` | Milliseconds between two taps that still count as one double tap. |
| `key seekSeconds` | `cls number` | `10` | Seconds a double tap seeks in the left and right zones. |
| `key disableClickToPause` | `cls boolean` | `false` | Stop the center zone's single tap from toggling playback. |

`key disableClickToPause` reaches the center zone's single tap and nothing else. The double tap there still enters fullscreen, and the seek and volume zones are untouched.

`cls DesktopUiPlugin` carries a field of the same name for its own click handler on the video element. The two are separate handlers on separate layers, so a build that wants neither sets both.

## The grid

Three columns by six rows. What fills them depends on one capability check taken at startup: `str ontouchstart` on `cls window`, or `navigator.maxTouchPoints` above zero.

Without touch, three columns span the full height:

| Column | One tap | Two taps |
|---|---|---|
| Left | Dismiss the controls when they were up at the moment of the tap. | Seek back `key seekSeconds`. |
| Center | Toggle playback. | Toggle fullscreen. |
| Right | Dismiss the controls when they were up at the moment of the tap. | Seek forward `key seekSeconds`. |

With touch, the middle column is split three ways and the outer columns give up their top row:

| Zone | Rows it declares | Two taps |
|---|---|---|
| Left and right | 2 to 6 | Seek back or forward. |
| Center, upper | 1 and 2 | Volume up. |
| Center, middle | 3 to 5 | Toggle fullscreen. |
| Center, lower | 5 and 6 | Volume down. |

Row 5 is declared twice. The lower volume zone is appended after the middle one, so it takes the tap there and the middle zone is two rows deep in practice.

Row 1 of the outer columns carries no zone at all, which leaves the top bar's own buttons a clear strip.

A single tap in either volume zone behaves like the seek zones: it dismisses the controls if they were showing, and does nothing if they were not.

## Seek feedback

Each seek zone grows a round indicator on its first use: a chevron pair and a running total. Three quick taps forward therefore read as 30 seconds rather than three separate tens.

The total goes back to zero one second after the last tap in that direction, and the indicator fades 200 ms later. It is placed against the video's own vertical center rather than the container's, so letterboxing does not push it off the frame.

## Rules & restrictions

- The single-tap decision reads a snapshot of control visibility taken at `str pointerdown` in the capture phase, ahead of `cls DesktopUiPlugin`'s own bubble-phase wake handlers.
- Without that snapshot a tap from hidden would show the controls and then hide them again a moment later, and the tap would look like it did nothing.
- The capability check runs once, at registration. A touch-capable laptop driven with a mouse gets the phone grid, volume zones included.
- The plugin emits `player.emit('activity', { active: false })` and never the true half. Waking the chrome is `cls DesktopUiPlugin`'s own job, and this plugin has no chrome to show.
- Zone geometry is internal. A different layout, a five-zone grid or a corner gesture, is a plugin of its own built on the same player methods: `fn rewind`, `fn forward`, `fn togglePlayback`, `fn toggleFullscreen`, `fn volumeUp` and `fn volumeDown`.

## How to extend it

Register it with the two numbers your app wants:

```ts
import nmplayer from '@nomercy-entertainment/nomercy-video-player';
import { TouchZonesPlugin } from '@nomercy-entertainment/nomercy-video-player/plugins/touch-zones';

const player = nmplayer('player');

player.addPlugin(TouchZonesPlugin, {
  seekSeconds: 15,
  doubleTapThreshold: 250,
});

player.setup({
  baseUrl: 'https://raw.githubusercontent.com/NoMercy-Entertainment/nomercy-media/master/Films',
  playlist: [
    {
      id: 'sintel',
      title: 'Sintel',
      url: '/Sintel.(2010)/Sintel.(2010).NoMercy.m3u8',
      duration: 888,
    },
  ],
});

await player.ready();
```

A longer `key doubleTapThreshold` buys a slower hand more time and costs every single tap the same delay, because the single-tap action waits out the window before it runs.
