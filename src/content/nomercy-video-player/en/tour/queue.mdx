# The Queue

A video queue is the kit's queue.

`queue()`, `item()` and every list method behave as [Core: The Queue](/nomercy-player-core/tour/queue) describes them.
This player changes none of it.

What is video's own is where playback opens, when the cursor moves without you, what is fetched before it moves, and what the player rewrites on the way in.

## Where playback opens

Every `setup()` with a non-empty playlist opens an item once `ready()` resolves.
That happens whether or not you set `autoPlay`, so the player never mounts blank.
`autoPlay` only decides whether the item it opened also starts playing.

Which item, and at what second, comes from each item's `progress`.
[Playlist items](/nomercy-video-player/reference/playlist-item) has that field and the rule it follows.

A playlist where nothing carries `progress` opens the first item, from the start.
[Persistence and resume](/nomercy-video-player/recipes/resume-playback) has the round trip that fills it.

### Your own choice wins

Call `item(target)` between `setup()` and `ready()` and the player keeps that choice.
The progress pick is skipped, and no resume offset is applied.

That is what lets a deep link open the episode in the URL rather than the one watched most recently.

## When the cursor moves on its own

At the natural end of an item the player calls `next()` itself.
This is on by default, and `autoAdvance: false` turns it off.

Turn it off when something else already drives `next()`, such as a Cast session or a socket-driven one.
Two things advancing one queue moves it two items.

The music player has no equivalent default. Auto-advance there is a plugin you register.

## What is fetched before the cut

Ten seconds before the current item ends, the player asks for the next item's assets.

`VideoPreloadStrategy` decides what those are: the media URL, the poster, the preview sprite, each subtitle sidecar, chapter sidecars and ASS font manifests.
`preloadLeadSeconds` moves the ten seconds.

Each asset gets one `HEAD` request, and the run announces itself through `preloadStart`, `preloadProgress` and `preloadComplete`.
The move between two items is a hard cut.

## Items are reshaped on the way in

Every item passes through `normalizePlaylistItem` as it enters the queue, so a media server payload goes in as it arrives.
`file` becomes `url`, a duration string becomes seconds, millisecond chapter cues become seconds, and a `thumbnails` track becomes `previewSpriteUrl`.

The pass is not a no-op on canonical items.
`fonts` is rebuilt from each entry's `file` alone, so a `label` on a font does not survive it.
Every other canonical field is carried through as you wrote it.
