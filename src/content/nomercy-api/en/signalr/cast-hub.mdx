# Cast hub

```text
wss://{server-address}:7626/castHub?access_token={token}
```

This hub carries both halves of casting: the app telling a receiver what to do, and the receiver saying what it is doing.

## Choosing a receiver and starting

```text
SelectChromecast(name)
Launch()
CastPlaylist(value)
Stop()
Disconnect()
```

Pick a receiver by name, launch on it, then hand it what to play. `Stop` ends playback; `Disconnect` gives the receiver back.

## Transport

```text
Play()
Pause()
SetSeek(time)
SetVolume(volume)
SetMuted(muted)
SetPlaylistItem(item)
```

## Tracks

```text
SetAudioTrack(audioTrack)
SetSubtitleTrack(subtitleTrack)
```

## What the receiver reports

The receiver uses the same connection to say what it has:

```text
Time(time)
Ended()
Volume(volume)
Muted(muted)
Item(item)
Playlist(items)
AudioTracks(tracks)
CurrentAudioTrack(track)
SubtitleTracks(tracks)
CurrentSubtitleTrack(track)
PlayerState(state)
```

`Time` carries the position, the duration, how far through it is and how much is left, so a controller draws a progress bar without doing arithmetic of its own.

## Asking where things stand

```text
GetPlayerState()
```

For a controller that joined part way through, rather than one that has been listening from the start.
