# Drives hub

```text
wss://{server-address}:7626/drivesHub?access_token={token}
```

A listening connection. The server pushes drive changes to it: a disc inserted, a disc ejected, a drive appearing or going away.

There is nothing to call on this hub. Connect and receive.

That is the point of it: a dashboard open on the disc page reacts the moment a disc goes in, without polling the drive and without spinning it up to ask.

For the state of one drive on demand, ask the [ripper hub](/nomercy-api/signalr/ripper-hub) instead.
