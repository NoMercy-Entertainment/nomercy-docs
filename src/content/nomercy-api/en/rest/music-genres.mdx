# Music genres

Music genres are separate from video genres, because they come from a different source. See [Genres](/nomercy-api/rest/genres) for video.

| Method | Path | Needs |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/api/v1/music/genres` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/music/genres/letter/{letter}` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/music/genres/{id}` | MediaAccess |

The listing takes `page` and `take`. The letter form browses by first letter, with `#` for anything that does not start with one.
