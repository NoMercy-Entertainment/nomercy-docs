# People

People are the cast and crew of what your server holds. The id is the TheMovieDB person id.

| Method | Path | Needs |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/api/v1/person` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/person/{id}` | MediaAccess |

The listing takes `page` and `take`. See [Pagination](/nomercy-api/pagination).

A person page combines two sources: the biography and images come from [TheMovieDB](https://www.themoviedb.org), and the credits are the titles your own server holds.

Because the details are fetched live, a person your server knows about can still answer 404 while TheMovieDB is unreachable.

If the server is set to hide adult content, a person marked adult is refused rather than returned.
