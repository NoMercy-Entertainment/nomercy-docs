# Genres

Video genres, and the titles on this server filed under one. Music has its own genres: see [Music Genres](/nomercy-api/rest/music-genres).

| Method | Path | Needs |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/api/v1/genres` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/genres/{genreId}` | MediaAccess |

Both take `page` and `take`. See [Pagination](/nomercy-api/pagination).

The id is the TheMovieDB genre id, so the same number means the same genre everywhere.

## Anime

Anime carries extra groupings of its own, addressed the same way.

| Method | Path | Needs |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/api/v1/anime/demographics` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/anime/demographics/{demographicId}` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/anime/seasons` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/anime/seasons/{seasonId}` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/anime/themes` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/anime/themes/{themeId}` | MediaAccess |

An anime season here means the broadcast season, such as a year and a quarter, not a season of one series.
