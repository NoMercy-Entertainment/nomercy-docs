# Recommendations

Suggestions of titles you do not have, worked out from what you do. They are separated by kind, because the reasoning differs.

| Method | Path | Needs |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/api/v1/dashboard/recommendations/movies` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/dashboard/recommendations/tv` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/dashboard/recommendations/anime` | MediaAccess |
| GET | `/api/v1/dashboard/recommendations/{type}/{id}` | MediaAccess |

Each listing takes `take` to cap how many come back.

The detail route returns one suggestion in full, so a client can show why it was suggested before you act on it.

## Diagnostics

| Method | Path | Needs |
|--------|------|-------|
| GET | `/api/v1/dashboard/recommendations/diagnostics` | Moderator |

Explains what the recommender had to work with. Useful when the suggestions look thin: usually the answer is that the library is small or one-sided.
