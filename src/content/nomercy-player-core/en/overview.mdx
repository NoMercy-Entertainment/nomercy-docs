# What is the NoMercy Player Core

[`nomercy-player-core`](/nomercy-player-core/quickstart) is the shared, headless engine the video and music players are built on. The two players are mostly the same machine underneath — a queue is a queue, auth is auth, and a plugin system does not care whether it is driving a film or an album — so that machine lives here once, and a fix in it lands in both players at the same time.

It handles the parts neither player should reimplement — the queue and transport, the auth pipeline, the plugin system and typed event bus, i18n, and storage — and then gets out of your way.

The important word is *headless*. There is nothing medium-specific here: no video backend, no audio graph, no UI. Those live in the player packages above. You never run the core on its own — you compose its mixins to build a player, or it arrives as a dependency of `nomercy-video-player` and `nomercy-music-player`.

## What you get

- Transport and a [queue engine](/nomercy-player-core/queue) — play, pause, seek, next, shuffle, backlog.
- The [auth pipeline](/nomercy-player-core/auth-fetch) — bearer tokens, 401 refresh, 403 propagation.
- The [plugin system](/nomercy-player-core/plugins) and a typed [event bus](/nomercy-player-core/event-system).
- [i18n](/nomercy-player-core/i18n) and [storage](/nomercy-player-core/storage).
- An [adapter port](/nomercy-player-core/adapters) for every cross-cutting concern, swappable without subclassing.
- Full TypeScript types for every method, event, and option.
