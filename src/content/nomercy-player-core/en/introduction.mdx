# What is Player Core

Install `@nomercy-entertainment/nomercy-player-core` on its own only when you are writing a plugin, or when you are building a player of your own.
If you want to play video, use the [video player](/nomercy-video-player).
If you want to play music, use the [music player](/nomercy-music-player).

Those packages already depend on Player Core, so you do not install it again.
Player Core is the engine they share: the queue, signed requests, plugins, and typed events.
It does not draw a picture or play sound by itself.

Video and music need that same engine.
Keeping it in one package means a fix lands once, for both.
A player you build starts the same way: compose this engine, then add the piece that is specific to your medium.

Nothing runs until you ask for it.
No plugin is active until you add it.
You can pass your own storage, logger, or similar piece into `setup`, and everything you leave out keeps its default.
