# What is Player Core

`@nomercy-entertainment/nomercy-player-core` is Player Core.
It is the shared logic that `nomercy-music-player` and `nomercy-video-player` are built from.
It is not a music player or a video player by itself.

`nomercy-music-player` is the music library.
`nomercy-video-player` is the video library.
Each one adds the playback for its own medium.

The behavior they share lives in this package.

These names are the pieces of that shared spine.

The queue is the ordered list of items, and which item is current.
Transport is play, pause, and seek.
Time is the playback position and the duration.

Volume is the loudness, on a scale from 0 to 100.
Events are messages the player emits, such as a phase change, that you subscribe to.
Plugins are extra behavior you register on the player.

Adapters are swappable pieces, such as how events are delivered or how a stream is opened.

The package also ships the helpers around that spine.
Lifecycle helpers are `setup`, `ready`, and `dispose`.
Streams are how a media address is opened, including HLS.

A cue is a timed span with a start, an end, and a payload.
Errors are named failures the player raises, such as a missing mount element.
Auth is optional credentials on requests the player makes.
