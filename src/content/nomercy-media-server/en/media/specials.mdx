# Specials

A special is a collection you build yourself. It holds films and episodes together, in an order you choose, and appears in the app as its own thing to watch.

This is not the same as a show's Specials season. That is covered at the bottom of this page, because the two share a word and nothing else.

## What you can put in one

Films and episodes, mixed freely, in any order.

That mixing is the point. A run through a franchise can put the films between the episodes where they actually belong. A themed collection can pull one episode from each of six different shows.

## Creating one

Go to Specials in the dashboard and create one. Give it a title, and artwork if you want it to look right on the home screen: a poster, a backdrop and a logo.

Then use the playlist editor to add films and episodes and drag them into order.

## Watching one

A special appears in the app like any other title. Playing it plays its items in the order you set, one after the next.

Your position is remembered, so a long collection can be watched across several evenings.

## Sharing

Specials belong to the server, so everyone who can reach the server can see them. People can like a special and add it to their watch list, and that part is per person.

## Removing one

Delete it from the dashboard. Deleting a special removes the collection only. The films and episodes in it are untouched and stay in their libraries.

## A show's Specials season

Separately from all of this, TV shows often have a season 0 holding pilots, recaps and holiday episodes. NoMercy calls that season **Specials** by default, and you can change what it is called per library from the library's settings.

That setting only renames a season in a show. It has no connection to the collections described above.
