# NoMercy Connect

Connect lets one device play while another controls it. Start something on the TV and drive it from your phone, or pick up on your phone what the TV was playing.

## How it works

At any moment one of your devices is the active one. It is the device actually playing. Everything else showing that session is passive: it mirrors what is happening and can send commands.

The server decides which device is active, rather than the devices negotiating between themselves. That is what keeps two clients from both believing they are in charge, and it is why the controls on your phone match the TV rather than drifting from it.

## Choosing a device

The device picker lists what you can play on. It shows devices that are connected right now, and it also shows devices the server knows about that are currently asleep, so a TV that is off is still something you can send playback to.

Picking a device makes it the active one. Your current device becomes a remote for it.

## What you can control

Play and pause, seek, skip forward and back, volume and mute, and the queue.

The queue is shared, so reordering on your phone changes what the TV plays next.

## Taking over

You can move playback the other way, back onto the device you are holding. It picks up where the other device was.

## Casting is separate

Chromecast is its own path. Casting hands playback to a Chromecast device, which is not a NoMercy client and is controlled differently.

The distinction matters when something misbehaves. Connect is NoMercy device to NoMercy device. Casting is NoMercy to a Chromecast.

::Troubleshooting

**A device is missing from the picker.**
It has to be signed in with the same account. A TV that has never been signed in does not appear.

**The TV is listed but does not respond.**
It may be asleep. The server can wake devices that support it; ones that do not need waking by hand.

**Controls on the phone do not match the TV.**
Leave and re-enter the session on the phone. It re-reads the current state from the server.

::
