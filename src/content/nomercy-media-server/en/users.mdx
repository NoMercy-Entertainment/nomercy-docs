# Users

A media server is usually for a household rather than one person. This page covers inviting people and deciding what each of them can do.

## Inviting someone

Open the dashboard, go to Users, and choose to invite. You need their email address, and you can pick which libraries they get while you are there.

The invitation goes to that email. They accept it with a NoMercy account, creating one if they do not have one, and your server picks them up shortly afterwards.

You are inviting an account, not creating one. Their account is theirs, works on their own devices, and you never handle their password.

## Who can do what

Access is a set of switches per person rather than a single rank.

| Setting | Gives them |
|---------|------------|
| Allowed | Access to content at all |
| Manage | The dashboard, so they can administer the server |
| Optical access | Use of a disc in the drive: browsing and watching it |
| Transcoding | Whether the server may convert for their device while they watch |

Beneath those, each person has a list of libraries they can see. You can give someone everything, or hand a child an Anime library and nothing else.

## The owner

One account owns the server: yours. That is fixed, and the owner cannot be removed.

Only the owner can add people, remove people, and grant someone the ability to manage the server. Someone with Manage can run the dashboard day to day but cannot promote anyone else, which keeps administration from spreading by accident.

Nobody can edit their own permissions, including you. This is deliberate: it means a mistake cannot lock you out of your own server.

## Optical access, separately

Access to a disc in the drive is its own switch because it means something different from library access. It lets a person browse and watch what is in the drive. It does not let them rip, eject, or change how ripping works, which stay with the people who manage the server.

## Removing someone

Remove them from the Users screen. Their access ends: they stop being able to reach your content and your libraries stop appearing for them.

Removal ends access to your server. It does not touch their NoMercy account, which is theirs and unaffected.

## One account, one person

There are no separate profiles inside an account. Each person gets their own account, and their watch progress, preferences and devices are their own.

That is a deliberate difference from services where a household shares one login and picks a face at the start. Here everyone signs in as themselves, on their own devices, and nobody resumes into someone else's episode.

::Troubleshooting

**They accepted the invitation but do not see the server.**
Your server takes a short while to pick up the change. If it persists, confirm they signed in with the same email you invited.

**They can sign in but see nothing.**
Check their library list. Someone with access to no libraries has an empty home screen rather than an error.

**They cannot play anything on one device.**
Check their transcoding settings. A device that needs conversion cannot play anything if conversion is switched off for that person.

::
