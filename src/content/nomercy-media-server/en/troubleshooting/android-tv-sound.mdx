# No sound on Android TV with a soundbar

Use this order when video plays in the NoMercy Android TV app but the soundbar is silent. Change one setting at a time and replay the same title after each step.

## 1. Check where sound is going

1. Confirm the soundbar is on and set to its TV or HDMI ARC input.
2. Check that the HDMI cable runs from the TV's ARC or eARC port to the soundbar's ARC or eARC port. A regular HDMI input may carry video without returning TV audio to the bar.
3. In the TV's sound settings, select the soundbar or HDMI ARC/eARC output. Test another app on the same Android TV box. If that app is silent too, fix the TV-to-soundbar route before changing NoMercy settings.

## 2. Change the box's digital audio output

This is the usual cause when a box sends sound through the TV to a soundbar.

An Android TV box has a setting called **Digital audio output**. On **Auto**, the box can convert sound to Dolby Digital Plus before it sends it to the TV.

A TV that passes sound to the soundbar over plain ARC often can't carry Dolby Digital Plus, and the TV can't always convert it. The result is video with no sound, or sound that cuts in and out.

eARC can carry it, so the same box may work on a newer TV.

Change the setting on the box, not in NoMercy:

1. Press the home button on the box's remote and open **Settings**.
2. Open **Device Preferences**. Some boxes skip this screen and show **Sound** directly under **Settings**.
3. Open **Sound**.
4. Open **Digital audio output**.
5. Choose **Passthrough**. If the list has no Passthrough, choose **PCM**.
6. Go back to NoMercy and play the same title again.

Menu names differ per box brand. If you can't find these names, look for a sound or audio output screen with the choices Auto, Passthrough and PCM.

If **Passthrough** is still silent, choose **PCM**. PCM is plain sound that every TV and soundbar can play. If PCM works, the cable and the soundbar are fine, and the audio format was the problem.

### What the app does by itself

The NoMercy app on a TV checks this setting. When the box is on **Auto**, the app shows a notice once with the title **No sound over your soundbar?** It names the setting and tells you to choose Passthrough.

Press **Got it** to close it. The notice does not come back after that, so use the steps above if you closed it earlier.

The app also turns stereo Dolby sound into plain sound before it reaches the box's output. A title with a stereo Dolby track plays on any setting. A title with a surround Dolby track still depends on the setting above.

Do not assume that a Dolby label on the title means every device in the HDMI chain can play that exact audio format. Test a second title with a different audio track before changing a server encoding profile.

## 3. Check volume and mute

Use the TV or soundbar remote to unmute and raise the soundbar volume. On an ARC-connected TV, the soundbar owns the volume level. The NoMercy Android client sends relative volume steps for this route; setting an absolute app volume may not change the amplifier.

If the on-screen volume indicator moves but there is still no sound, return to the output and format checks above.

## 4. Isolate the failing link

Try the same title on another NoMercy client, then try another title on this Android TV box. If only one title is silent, select a different audio track for that title.

If every title is silent only on the TV box while other apps have sound, check the box's digital audio output in step 2 again, then restart playback. If every app is silent, inspect the box, TV, cable, and soundbar settings.

For a playback failure that includes an error or buffering, see [Common issues](/nomercy-media-server/troubleshooting/common-issues).
