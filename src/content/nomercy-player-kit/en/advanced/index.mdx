# Advanced

Read this section when you need to go beyond the built-in capabilities — replacing infrastructure, running in non-browser environments, or tuning for production scale.

**Prerequisites:** You should be comfortable with the [architecture](/player/architecture) and have a working player from one of the quickstarts before diving in here.

---

| Guide                                                                         | What it covers                                                                                                                 |

## When to read recipes first

If you haven't built the common setups yet, go to [Recipes](/player/recipes/) first. The advanced guides assume you know how to wire subtitles, chapters, auth, and queue management.
